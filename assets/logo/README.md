# Pluto's logo

`pluto.svg` is Pluto's logo: an amber planet with rounded lobes, circled by a ring. A vector
outline clips a raster texture embedded in the file, which is why it weighs about 1 MB.

It is not under the code's license, MIT: its author keeps its rights
([ADR-0013](../../docs/adr/0013-plutos-logo.md)). You may show it, unchanged, to refer to Pluto: in
an article, a talk, or a page that links to it. You may not use it, or something close to it, as
the logo of another project, a fork of Pluto included, nor so as to suggest that a project is Pluto
or endorsed by it.

The applications' icon is still `assets/icon.svg`; the logo replaces it when the client moves to
uwui.
